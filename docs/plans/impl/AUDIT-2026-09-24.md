# Audit: WP-01 … WP-19 vs the plans (2026-09-24)

Read-only audit of the code, commits, logs and deploy state against each WP
spec and its "Notes from implementation". Nothing was changed while auditing.
Verdicts: ✅ solid · ⚠️ partial · ❌ broken / off track.

## Headline

The code for most WPs exists. What slipped is the **process**:

- The "Mario reviews each WP live before the next one builds on it" gate
  stopped being used after WP-08. WP-09 through WP-19 were stacked on top of
  each other in about 36 hours. Most were never seen working live, and none
  are ticked.
- Notes regularly present unit or smoke tests as "live-verified".
- **Nothing from WP-08 onward is pushed**: felt-animation is 15 commits
  ahead, felt-tray 6, felt-dashboard 2, and the umbrella repo 22.
- WP-18C's main.gd work only exists inside four generic
  "Update Felt assets" commits.

## Urgent (live problems right now)

1. **Privacy: the Felt chat window can be captured and sent to the cloud.**
   The `felt-tray.exe` self-exclusion never matches, because the uv launcher
   runs `python.exe`. That window shows local-only replies (documents and
   facts). (WP-07)
2. **Privacy: UWP apps get past the blocklist.** The
   ApplicationFrameHost child lookup the spec required was skipped. For
   example, Settings was sent to Gemma on 09-23. (WP-07)
3. **Screenshots are piling up.** `FELT_CAPTURE_DEBUG_DIR` is still set in
   `felt-tray/.env`, and `F:\Temp\felt-capture-debug` holds 200 JPEGs
   (93 of them from Endfield), still growing. (WP-07)
4. **Server error loop.** felt-core's `music_taste_loop` throws
   `NOT NULL constraint failed: music_taste.track_id` every 15 s (about 40
   errors per 10 min, 2,850 per day). It gets stuck on a Spotify item with a
   null id. (WP-16)
5. **Lipsync is probably zeroed every frame.** `_process_face_regions`
   rewrites every `Fcl_MTH_*` shape right after lipsync sets them
   (main.gd ~1048-1055 vs 1149-1167). Needs one live look to confirm.
   (WP-12)
6. **Nothing Felt-side is running.** felt-tray has been down since 09-23
   22:22, and the felt-animation restart was killed (low memory).

## WP-19: where it went off track

The WP's core deliverable is the Orpheus non-verbals. None exist.

- **Step 5 (hook-up) was built first**, at 18:56 in felt-animation `4925036`,
  before any audio existed.
- **Step 2 fillers (15 WAVs)** went through felt-voice's real `/v1/tts`
  path, so they are in the one voice. The generation script wasn't saved.
- **Step 1 Orpheus** was dropped after a llama.cpp Vulkan download on the
  desktop (120 MB in `tools/llamacpp/`, untracked and not ignored). There is
  no model, no SNAC decoder and no script, and nothing was tried on the
  Nitro5. No reason was written down.
- **Steps 3 and 4** exist (`revoice_batch.py` on the Nitro5, which overwrites
  in place; `curate.html`), but there is nothing to feed them.
- **The WP-19 section has no notes at all.** Its `## WP-19` heading was
  deleted by `ca8b5f7`, which breaks the README link.
- It ran in the same session right after WP-18C, which breaks the
  one-WP-per-session rule.
- **Step-5 bugs, for when keepers exist:**
  - a tagged reply cuts off her own sentence, because `player.start()`
    stops the reply audio;
  - presence lines ("back!", "rude!", "zip it") are mixed into the
    slow-request filler pool;
  - there's no giggle mapping;
  - `FELT_SOUNDS_DIR` isn't in `.env.example`.

**Per spec, the next step** is Orpheus on the Nitro5 (CUDA). Stop Ollama and
ComfyUI, make 3–5 takes of each of the 10 non-verbals, and restart the
services. Then run them through `revoice_batch.py` into a copy, rebuild
`curate.html`, and Mario picks. Fix the step-5 bugs only after that.

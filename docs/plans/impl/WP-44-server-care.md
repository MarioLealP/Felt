# WP-44 — Server and PC care: Felt as the first responder to alerts

Board: FELT "WP-44". Depends on WP-41 (alert hygiene, done) and the WP-42 log.
Source of the decisions: WP-42 decisions log, 10-03 (moved here as the plan).

## What it is

Everything that publishes to the clockmost ntfy topic (Zabbix, Wazuh, the
backup / deploy / health scripts, CI) reaches Felt first. She is the first
responder:

- for every alert she tells Mario three things: **is it expected**, **is
  there anything he can do**, **who's on it** (Mario 10-03: "mostly I need her
  to tell me if this is a warning that is meant to be happening and if there
  is something i can do kind of deal");
- expected events (a deploy, Renovate, a reboot, the PC being off) are
  **told, never acted on**;
- concerning ones, security or not, are **Claude's to handle** (WP-58: a
  headless Claude on the server, investigate-only to start; server changes
  wait for Mario's go-ahead; the Claude session is kept so Mario can join it);
- notifications **pop everywhere** (phone and desktop alike).

ntfy stays only as the server-side topic. Its phone app is retired by the
phone app WP (42.6), not here (Mario 10-03: "The phone app isn't a priority
so unless we on the phone app WP, just leave it to then").

Per-type rules (tier, handler, how long to wait for a RESOLVED):
[alert-catalogue.md](alert-catalogue.md).

## Order (slow, steady, in order — Mario 10-03)

1. **WP-44 — intake and rules (felt-core).** Every ntfy alert becomes an
   incident: its catalogue type, open/resolved (a RESOLVED closes the alert
   it answers), the wait-before-it-counts for self-fixing types, repeats
   folded into one. Deterministic, no model.
2. **WP-44 — her line.** The three-part line in her voice, generated by her
   12B; tested on the real past alerts first, so only the types she gets
   right are hers.
3. **WP-58 — the Claude handler.** She hands concerning incidents over; the
   headless Claude investigates read-only and reports back to the incident.
4. **WP-42.5 — the Systems page + desktop notifications.** Incidents with her
   line and Claude's report; "handled", "ask her about it".
5. **WP-42.6 — the phone app** (later, its own WP): notifications on the
   phone, then the ntfy app goes.

Until 5, the ntfy app keeps working exactly as now.
